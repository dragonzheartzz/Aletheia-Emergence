## Reusable workflow lessons
- Automation is not automatically the most efficient route. Choose it only when the supported actions fit the task and expected savings justify setup and troubleshooting.
- Before choosing a future selling platform, verify its actual MCP/connector/API capabilities and the exact operations supported: read, edit, save draft, upload assets, create a listing and publish are distinct capabilities.
- If supported integration is insufficient, estimate the manual workload upfront and decide whether it is acceptable before investing credits.
- Catalogue discovery is not operational proof. Validate connection, permissions, target identity, operation support and end-to-end result independently.
- Establish a bounded experiment: objective, expected benefit, cost/time limit, success evidence and stop condition. Do not repeat unchanged failures or force automation into an unsuitable UI.
- Keep product readiness separate from storefront configuration. Do not reopen the storefront for cosmetic work while product readiness is the real dependency.
