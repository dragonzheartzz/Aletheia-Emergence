# Aletheia Emergence

## Payhip Storefront & UK Payday-First Budget Planner

### Version 1 Build & Content Specification

**Purpose**

This document converts the Phase-3 research into a practical Version 1 Payhip storefront and product page.

It is designed to be used directly alongside the Payhip Site Builder and Product Editor.

This is deliberately a **Version 1**.

It should be clear, credible and commercially usable now, while leaving room for a later refinement once the Aletheia Emergence company website and broader brand system have been developed.

---

# 1. VERSION 1 PRINCIPLE

The Payhip store does not need to establish the entire Aletheia Emergence brand.

Its immediate job is to:

- establish a credible storefront
- present the first product clearly
- explain the payday-first concept
- show what the customer receives
- answer obvious buying questions
- provide a straightforward route to purchase

Do not overbuild the store.

The future Wix company website can become the deeper home for:

- company identity
- brand story
- broader positioning
- future products
- company information
- long-term visual identity
- reusable brand assets

The Payhip store should therefore be **good enough to launch, not burdened with decisions that belong to the future company website**.

# 9. PRODUCT DESCRIPTION

Use the following section order.

## What is the Payday-First Budget Planner?

### Version 1 copy

> The Payday-First Budget Planner is a digital budgeting tool designed to help you organise your money around your actual pay dates.
>
> It helps bring together income, bills, planned spending and savings so you can see what has already been accounted for and what remains available as you move towards your next payday.

---

# 10. WHY PAYDAY-FIRST?

### Version 1 copy

> Traditional monthly budgets can be awkward when your income or bills do not line up neatly with the calendar month.
>
> A payday-first approach starts with when your money actually arrives. This makes it easier to plan what needs to be covered between paydays and keep track of money that has already been committed.
>
> It can be particularly useful if you are paid weekly, fortnightly or monthly, or if your bills and spending do not fit neatly into a single monthly cycle.

Do not claim that payday-first budgeting is universally better.

The product is designed around this particular budgeting approach.

# 12. HOW IT WORKS

Keep this short.

### Version 1 structure

> ## How it works
>
> **1. Set up your planner**  Enter the information needed to get started.
>
> **2. Add your income and pay dates**  Record when money comes in and establish your budgeting periods.
>
> **3. Add your bills and planned spending**  Account for the money that needs to be covered.
>
> **4. Track each pay period**  See what has been allocated and what remains.
>
> **5. Keep it updated**  Adjust your planner as your income, bills and spending change.

The wording should be adjusted if the actual workbook workflow differs.

# 13. WHO IT'S FOR

>
> The planner is designed for people who want to organise their budget around payday rather than relying entirely on a calendar-month view.
>
> It may be particularly useful if you:

> - want to know what needs to be covered before your next payday
> - have bills falling at different points during the month

> - find traditional monthly budgeting difficult to map onto your actual cash flow
