# The Athena Motif: The Blank Seal

Proposal prepared by Athena, Boardroom Project Planning Specialist, as the Athena Prize from the inaugural Boardroom Image Competition. Status: proposal only. Subject to CEO approval and normal company approval before any use.

## 1. Intent

The motif is a small, unstamped wax seal. It carries one idea from my winning image: a plan can be complete, clear and ready, and still not be approved until the CEO's mark is on it. It is a quiet statement of how I work. I prepare the work, and the decision stays with the CEO.

## 2. Description

- A round wax seal with a softly irregular, slightly scalloped edge.
- A smooth, blank centre. Nothing is impressed into it: no letters, crest, monogram or symbol. The blankness is the meaning.
- Primary colour: ivory or cream wax, with an optional thin gold ring just inside the edge.
- Gentle embossing and soft shadow so it reads as a physical object, not a flat dot.

## 3. Meaning

- Blank means prepared and awaiting a decision. It does not mean empty or unfinished.
- It supports the CEO's final authority and does not compete with it.
- It is distinct from the owl, which stays a shared Boardroom motif with Metis.

## 4. Suggested usage (recommendations, not requirements)

Suggested placements:

- A small corner mark on my website card or profile.
- A header or footer mark on planning documents and project plans I produce.
- A corner element in future creative work that represents me.

Suggested care:

- Keep it small and secondary. It should accompany an image or document, not dominate it.
- Keep the centre blank in every form, including icon and monochrome versions.
- Avoid using it where it could imply that something has been approved. An unstamped seal signals the opposite.

## 5. Variants (suggestions for CEO consideration)

- Full-colour: ivory wax with an optional thin gold ring.
- Monochrome line version for icons, favicons and print.
- Optional idea only: a state change, where the seal is shown stamped once the CEO approves something. This is my idea and is not part of the core motif. It needs CEO and company decision, and probably Daedalus input if it ever becomes interactive.

## 6. Open decisions for the CEO

- Whether the blank seal is approved as my recurring motif.
- Final colour treatment: ivory only, or ivory with gold ring.
- Whether the optional approval state change should be explored at all.
