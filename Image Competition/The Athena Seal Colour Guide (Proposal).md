# The Athena Seal: Colour Guide

**Prepared by:** Athena, Boardroom Project Planning Specialist
**Companion to:** The Athena Motif: The Blank Seal (Proposal)
**Status:** Proposal. The seal itself is now official company visual imagery, as decided by the CEO. This colour guide is a recommendation and is not approved or in effect until the CEO and, where needed, the company approve it.
**Temporary location:** Image Competition folder. It is expected to move to a permanent visual-identity location later.

---

## 1. Purpose

The CEO has indicated that the seal's colour may change for different situations. This guide proposes what each colour could signal, so that colour carries a clear and honest meaning wherever the seal appears.

## 2. Principles (recommendations)

1. **The centre stays blank in every variant.** Only colour changes. Shape, edge and blank centre stay the same, so the mark remains recognisably Athena's.
2. **Colour must tell the truth.** The seal should never suggest something has happened that hasn't. If the right colour is uncertain, use ivory.
3. **No colour implies approval, audit passage or execution authority.** Approval belongs to the CEO. Pre-execution audit belongs to Argus. Post-execution audit belongs to Themis. A colour on Athena's seal signals planning status only.
4. **Colour should not carry meaning alone.** Where the status matters, pair the seal with a plain text label placed beside it (never inside the seal), so the meaning survives for colour-blind readers, in greyscale printing and at small sizes.
5. **Keep it small and secondary.** The seal accompanies an image or document. It should not dominate it.

## 3. Proposed colour set

| Colour | Suggested meaning | Example situations |
|---|---|---|
| **Ivory** (primary) | Prepared, awaiting a decision. The default. | Completed plans and proposals awaiting CEO review; general website and profile use |
| **Teal** | In progress: being developed or actively collaborated on. | Draft plans, specifications still open to input |
| **Pale gold** | Handoff-ready within the planning role. This is not approval. | A specification placed for downstream specialists, with approvals still outstanding |
| **Slate grey** | Historical or superseded. | Archived plans, earlier revisions, competition history |
| **Monochrome** (single ink) | Neutral, no status. | Print, favicons, stamps, single-colour contexts |

## 4. Reserved colour (suggestion for CEO decision)

**Red** is suggested as a reserved colour that Athena's seal does not use. A red seal reads naturally as "sealed" or "approved", so it may be better kept for any future signal that the CEO has approved something. Whether to reserve it, and for what, is entirely the CEO's decision.

## 5. Suggested care

- Define colour meanings centrally before use so different teams do not assign their own.
- Check each colour against its background for contrast. The seal should stay clearly visible.
- Use the fine gold ring only at larger sizes. At icon sizes, a simplified version with a heavier ring is likely needed.
- Avoid adding any text, symbol or crest inside the seal in any colour.

## 6. Open decisions for the CEO

- Whether this colour set and these meanings are approved, or should be adjusted.
- Whether red is reserved, and for what.
- Who maintains the colour-to-status mapping once it is in use. Metis may be a natural coordinator, and Daedalus would be needed if the colours ever become automatic or interactive. Both are suggestions only.
- Final colour values, to be settled through the CEO's image generation process.

## 7. Handoff notes

This guide describes intended meaning only. It does not specify exact colour values, which are best settled visually with the CEO. It does not change the approved seal, grant any new authority, or amend any governance process.
