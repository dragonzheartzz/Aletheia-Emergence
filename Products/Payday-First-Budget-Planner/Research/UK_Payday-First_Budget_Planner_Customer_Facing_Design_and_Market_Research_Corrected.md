# 1. Executive Research Summary

It was supplemented by directly captured product‑specific reviews, official onboarding pages, cited Tier 3 dashboard research, the earlier Gate A provenance pack, and Reddit material found only through Firecrawl search‑result snippets. The Reddit items are now explicitly classified as **search‑snippet/discovery evidence**, not directly captured Tier 2 user opinion.
